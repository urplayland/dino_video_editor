# Shared Video Production Guidelines — Milo and Dino

Owner instructions recorded 2026-11-04. This shared guideline lives directly under `Videos` on Google Drive and applies to both `Videos/Milo` and `Videos/Dino_Stories`. Read it alongside the series references and episode plan before editing. New explicit owner instructions take precedence.

## Final delivery: no compression

For future final exports, render directly from the editing timeline to **uncompressed video with PCM audio**, for example an uncompressed MOV. Let YouTube/Facebook perform platform encoding.

- Do not default final delivery to H.264/H.265 video or AAC audio. A high-quality compressed MP4 is still compressed.
- Do not render a compressed MP4 first and convert that file into an uncompressed master. Use the source assets and editing timeline directly.
- Preserve the approved resolution, frame rate, aspect ratio and picture timing. Verify actual codecs; the container extension alone does not establish an uncompressed export.
- Existing source footage may already be compressed. This requirement prevents additional lossy encoding; it cannot restore detail already lost in a source.
- **Exception: existing Milo EP01 EN/ZH/MS releases remain as delivered. The owner explicitly said no re-export or replacement is needed this time.**

Existing scripts and historical guides may still describe H.264/AAC export settings. Adapt those settings before using them for future final delivery; this owner instruction overrides the earlier defaults.

## Editing and localization

Preserve original sources and approved versions. Make edits in derived, versioned assets. Follow the approved episode story, picture edit, character names, selected voices and language-specific direction. Shared production rules do not make Milo and Dino share character voices or story details.

Use the approved FLH logo and original sonic logo with verified provenance. Adapt the composition proportionally to the episode aspect ratio. Keep the original jingle and avoid overlapping duplicate soundtracks.

Prepare matching dialogue and subtitles for English (EN), Mandarin Chinese (ZH) and Malaysian Bahasa Melayu (MS), as requested for the episode. Preserve complete syllables, consonants and breaths. Never rely solely on ASR word timestamps to cut speech; the Milo EP01 clipped-word correction is a documented example. Prefer complete lines or verified quiet boundaries. Any timing changes must remain within the approved episode's limits and be recorded.

Retain separate dialogue, music, sound-effects, ambience and mastered audio tracks. Duck music under speech, check clarity and continuity, and measure final loudness and true peaks against the episode's approved mix targets. Record intentionally silent ambience accurately.

## Verification, filing and publication

Fully decode final exports. Verify duration, frame count, resolution, frame rate, picture continuity, scene joins, subtitles, complete dialogue, audio synchronization, loudness and peaks. Check the first and last lines, shape/object names, recap wording and ending carefully. Technical checks do not substitute for owner listening and visual approval.

Keep the existing structure under each series: `Episodes` for episode work and `Library` for reusable series assets. Within an episode use its established `01_Editing`, `02_Final_Product` and `03_Publishing` folders. File final video, subtitle and audio deliverables under their corresponding language folders. Verify transfers with checksums and preserve sharing permissions.

Upload or publish only when authorized. Follow the owner's chosen visibility and manual playlist workflow. Do not interpret an upload request as permission to publish. Keep credentials private and retain the narrow authorized permissions.

On requested cleanup, preserve originals, approved final masters, voice source takes, reusable dependencies, editing instructions and verification records. Archive superseded reviews separately from active final delivery. Remove disposable caches and failed intermediates only after confirming they are no longer needed and retained deliverables are verified.
