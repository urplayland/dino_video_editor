# Dino Saves the Moon — production guidelines

Preserve the original picture, owner-approved edit/performances and selected voices. Do not regenerate animation or fabricate transcript/timestamps.

- Use the shared FLH palette/logo and current versioned branding/voice/SFX registries. Shared assets are referenced, not copied into new ad hoc kits.
- Retain original sources, exact selected voice-generation metadata and editable stems. No paid generation without explicit authorization.
- EN/ZH/MS are separate full videos with matching full-frame outlined/shadowed subtitles. Audio is timing authority. Natural Malaysian Malay and canonical character names apply.
- Music/ambience/SFX support the narrative and are ducked beneath speech. Final mixing/geometry follows the frozen episode configs, not guessed settings.
- Final_Product contains Videos,Subtitles,Audio,Clean_Master. Publishing contains only episode-specific covers/copy/checklist/website handoff. Channel branding is shared.
- Status and selected current links belong in episode-status.json and EPISODE_INDEX.md. Never mark a video published without a verified URL.
